Try Dialectic

A class reads together: highlight a passage, ask about it, and your classmates answer first. AI only steps in as far as the class charter allows. No passwords: pick any person.

On the web (any device)

Open Dialectic
  1. Pick a person (try Ana, a student, or Prof. Rivera).
  2. Open Week 1 · Hobbes. Select a sentence, then Ask or Note.
  3. Highlights in the margin are the class's notes. Table shows who wrote where.

iPhone and iPad

Open the web link above in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen for a full-screen app.

The native app (Apple Pencil highlighting, Ask from any app) is in TestFlight; a public install link is waiting on Apple's beta review. On iPad with the app: drag the Pencil across a line like a highlighter, lift, and Ask | Note opens. Fingers scroll.

Mac app, with the hotkey

Download for Mac
  1. Unzip and move Dialectic to Applications. It's a test build, so run this once in Terminal before opening it:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Dialectic.app
  2. Open it. It lives in the menu bar. Pick who you are in its menu.
  3. Allow Accessibility and Screen Recording when asked (System Settings → Privacy & Security), then reopen it.
  4. Open a course reading anywhere, for example Week 1 · Hobbes from the web link above, in your browser. Select a passage and press ⌃ ⌥ D (Control + Option + D).
  5. Ask a question or leave a note. It lands in the class's margin on the right page, and the answer shows classmates first.

macOS 14 or later, Intel or Apple Silicon. Turn on Highlights in the panel to see the class's notes drawn over the page you're reading.
